How We Write

Every idea on this site comes from real research or a named book — Tversky & Kahneman on anchoring, Cialdini on influence, Ariely on the decoy and IKEA effects, Thaler on sunk costs, Godin on remarkable marketing, Kim & Mauborgne on blue oceans — and every article lists its sources. We explain the study in plain words, show where you meet the idea in everyday life, and say plainly where the evidence is strong and where it is only suggestive.

We do not sell courses, consulting, or marketing services, and no company pays us to recommend anything. Our only goal is to explain the psychology clearly enough that you can spot it as a shopper — or use it honestly as a seller.

One idea at a time

Each article covers a single concept, so you can read one in about seven minutes and actually remember it. If you are new here, start with the anchoring effect — it is the idea that makes almost every other pricing trick work.
