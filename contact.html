Contact Us

Have a question about an article, spotted an error, or want us to cover a topic? We would like to hear from you.

Reach Us on GitHub

Send us a message through our GitHub repository — open an issue there and we will reply. We read every message and aim to reply within a few business days.

Please do not send personal information

Never send us passwords, payment details, or any other personal information. We do not need it to answer a question, and GitHub issues are public — anything you write there can be seen by anyone. Keep your message general.

What We Can and Cannot Help With

We can answer questions about the ideas on this site, correct mistakes, and take suggestions for topics to cover. We cannot give personal business, marketing, or financial advice, review your pricing or advertising, or recommend specific products or services. For decisions about your own business, please speak with a qualified professional.

Corrections

If you believe an article contains an error, a misattributed study, or an out-of-date claim, please tell us which article and which point, and if you can, the original source you are comparing it with. We check every report against the original source and correct confirmed errors promptly.

Disclaimer: Mindfluence provides general information about marketing psychology and consumer behaviour. Nothing on this site is financial, legal, or professional advice. Studies cited are summarised for general readers; check the original research before relying on any finding for a business decision.
